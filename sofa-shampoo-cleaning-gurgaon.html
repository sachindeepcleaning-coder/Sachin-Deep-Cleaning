About Sofa Shampoo Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Your sofa takes a daily beating — drinks spilled, kids climbing, pets curling up, and fine Gurgaon dust settling deep into the fabric. Over time, that invisible buildup turns fabric dull, triggers allergies, and traps odors that air fresheners simply mask.

Our sofa shampoo cleaning uses a hot-water extraction and low-moisture method to flush out embedded dirt, dust mites and allergens, lift stains and neutralize odors. We first assess the fabric — cotton, linen, jacquard, velvet or leather — and choose the right treatment. Leather sofas receive a gentle conditioner-safe clean instead of shampoo. Prices start at ₹499 per seat for dry cleaning and ₹599 per seat for shampoo extraction. A 3-seater usually takes 1.5–2 hours and dries in 4–6 hours, ready for the evening.

How much does sofa cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Sofa cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹499 per seat for dry cleaning and ₹599 per seat for hot-water shampoo extraction. A standard 3-seater sofa comes to ₹1,500–₹1,800, and a corner 5–6 seater runs ₹2,200–₹2,800. Stain and odour treatment is included in every seat price, and a recliner is ₹199.

What is the price difference between dry cleaning and shampoo cleaning a sofa?

Dry cleaning is a deep vacuum with a spot treatment spray, which lifts loose dust but leaves ground-in soil inside the foam. Shampoo cleaning pushes heated shampoo into the fabric and pulls it back out with the dirt, for ₹599 a seat against ₹499 for dry cleaning. Shampoo needs four to six hours of drying.

How long does a sofa take to dry after shampoo cleaning?

Four to six hours for a standard sofa, and six to eight hours for a corner unit or a thickly padded one. We use low-moisture extraction and place air movers where the sofa sits, so it is usually ready to sit on by evening and fully usable the next morning. Covering it while drying helps, but is not required.

How much does a 3-seater sofa cleaning cost?

A standard 3-seater sofa costs ₹1,500–₹1,800 to shampoo in Gurgaon, which works out to ₹500–₹600 per seat. The price holds for sofas with three seat cushions and two back cushions. Corner units with fixed panels or extra chaise sections are quoted individually, and a fabric protection spray adds ₹150.

Sofa cleaning ke baad use kab kar sakte hain?

Shampoo cleaning ke baad sofa ko 4 se 6 ghante use na karein — wet upholstery par nishan pad jaate hain aur shape bigad sakti hai. Dry cleaning me aisa nahi hota, wahan turant use kar sakte hain. Puri tarah sukha hone ke baad sofa ek din me normal jaisa lagta hai.

Is sofa shampoo cleaning safe for fabric and leather?

Yes, provided the right method is matched to the material. Cotton, linen, wool, nylon and polyester are shampooed with hot-water extraction after a colour-fastness test on a hidden patch. Leather and rexine are not shampooed at all — they are cleaned with a pH-neutral leather cleaner and conditioned afterwards to stop cracking.

Can you remove stains and pet odour from a sofa?

Yes. Spills, oil marks, ink and pet urine are pre-treated individually before extraction, and odour is neutralised rather than masked. Deep-set urine staining in foam sometimes leaves a shadow even after a full shampoo, and we tell you that on the call. Every sofa job ends with a deodorising pass, included in the per-seat price.
