Why It Matters

About Balcony Cleaning Services in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A Gurgaon balcony is an outdoor room that nobody owns. It collects the dust from every storm in April and May, the dried leaves from the trees along Sohna Road, pigeon droppings from every railing, and cooking smoke if it sits next to the kitchen. The floor goes grey and slippery, the drain blocks, and the moss in the corners turns the wall green. It is the first place a guest is taken to show the view, and usually the dirtiest surface in the home.

We start with a dry sweep to lift the loose dust and leaf litter, then apply a degreaser where smoke or food residue has stuck to the tiles. The floor is pressure-washed with a foam lance so the grout lines come out clean, and the drain mouth is opened and the line flushed until the water runs free. Railings, grills and mesh are scrubbed, glass is wiped down, and the corners get the cobweb and moss treatment. Every balcony is left draining, not just looking clean.

The price is ₹399 per balcony and each one takes 45–60 minutes. The price moves with the size of the balcony and the depth of the blockage in the drain line, with floor-to-ceiling glass that has to be reached from inside the room, and with balconies on an upper floor where the water has to be carried up. Two or more balconies in one home are done in one visit with the same crew.

Balcony cleaning runs across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1 and the New Colony towers where the balcony is the only open space in the flat. It also makes sense right before the rains, when the drains are still clear and the grout has not yet started growing moss. Pair it with a full home deep clean if the balcony has never been cleaned.

How much does balcony cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Balcony cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹399 per balcony for a pressure wash and scrub, ₹499 with the glass panel and railing, and ₹599 with pigeon-dropping treatment. Two balconies in the same flat are ₹699. The price is confirmed on the call after a photo.

How long does one balcony take to clean in Gurgaon?

A typical Gurgaon balcony takes 45 to 60 minutes for a pressure wash, scrub and drain flush. Adding the glass panel and railing takes about an hour in total, and pigeon-dropping treatment takes 75 minutes because the treatment needs its own contact time before removal.

Do you clear blocked balcony drains?

We flush and clear light silt from the drain as part of the wash, because that is the most common cause of a blocked balcony drain in Gurgaon. A blockage deeper in the pipe is a separate plumbing job, and we flag it on the tick sheet rather than pretend it is cleaned.

Can you remove pigeon droppings from a balcony?

Yes, at ₹599 with the balcony clean, which includes a treatment that loosens the droppings before removal. We wear protective gear and work from the outer edge where it is safe, and we do not treat a balcony where a droppings fall on a neighbour below without checking.

Is balcony cleaning worth it before the monsoon in Gurgaon?

Yes, and it is most valuable just before the monsoon rather than after. Dust, grit and dried pigeon droppings on a balcony tile block the drainage path, and that is what causes the overflow many Gurgaon flats see in the first heavy rain. A ₹399 wash before June is cheaper than water damage.

Do you clean the balcony of a society flat in Gurgaon?

Yes, and it is often booked as part of the full-home deep clean. A standalone balcony clean is ₹399, or ₹699 for two. For society buildings, the booking goes through the RWA window and the cost is a normal per-balcony rate with a GST invoice on request.
