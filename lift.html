About Lift & Elevator Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A lift cabin is the most-touched room in any building and the least cleaned. Shoe marks on the floor plate, handprints across the mirror, dust in the ceiling light diffuser and a dull film on the button panel that dozens of hands press daily. Landing floors collect the same treatment, and the door sill gathers the grit the rollers drag in with every trip. In the shaft and pit, dust, grease and old lubricant settle where nobody looks — and that is where a fault can start.

The cabin is cleaned with the lift on service mode and the doors held open. We lift the floor plate or mat, vacuum the track area, scrub the plate, and wipe the mirror, handrails, ceiling light and return panels. Buttons and the display are detailed without spraying liquid into the panel, and the doors are wiped where hands touch. Landing floors and call panels on every floor are done in one pass. The shaft and pit are inspected and cleaned with the lift vendor’s permission; the machine room stays with the maintenance company.

Lift and elevator cleaning is ₹1,499 per lift, covering the cabin, the landing floors and the shaft. One price covers a residential tower lift and a service lift in an office block, because the scope is the same three surfaces. What changes the number is the number of floors served, since every landing panel and sill is part of the job, and how long the cabin has gone uncleaned. Machine room and motor cleaning stay with the lift maintenance company.

We clean lifts in the residential towers of DLF Phase 1–5, Sushant Lok, Nirvana Country, Ardee City and the Sector 45–57 societies, and in the commercial lobbies of Cyber City, Udyog Vihar and Sohna Road. With several lifts, the schedule is planned so no floor goes long without service. If the building interior also needs work, the same visit can be paired with an office deep clean for the lobby around it.

How often should a lift be cleaned in Gurgaon?

Every three months for a residential tower and every month for a commercial building with public footfall. Gurgaon construction dust settles on cabin panels and door tracks within weeks, and the door channel is where the grit that scratches the floor collects.

Do you clean the lift floor and door tracks too?

Yes. The cabin floor is scrubbed and the door tracks are vacuumed and wiped, because grit in the channel grinds the floor the first time the door opens. A cabin clean in Gurgaon at ₹1,499 covers panels, floor, tracks, ceiling and the door faces, not just a mop over the floor.

Can you clean lifts in a society in Gurgaon?

Yes — a tower lift block covering every cabin plus every landing is from ₹4,999 for a full-day visit, with a per-lift tick sheet and a GST invoice. We book within the RWA window, carry IDs, and pre-share crew names for the gate pass so no slot is lost to process.

Do you polish stainless steel lift panels?

Yes, at ₹499 per cabin, which includes removing fingerprints, smears and hard-water spots without scratching the brushed finish. It is optional and only quoted when the panel is steel rather than enamel or laminate.

Do you clean lifts after hours in Gurgaon?

Yes, and after-hours or weekend slots are included at no extra charge. We schedule so no workday is lost and no resident queue forms — a cabin is cleaned inside a single window, with the landing lobby in the same visit.

What changes the lift cleaning price

  • Cabin count — a building with two or more lifts needs a per-cabin line each
  • Scope — cabin only, cabin plus landing lobby, or shaft-side landing
  • Society block — a tower-level visit is priced by crate to the tower, not per lift
  • Panel material — steel panels need the polish line, laminate does not
  • Access — gate passes, lift out-of-service windows are confirmed on the call
  • Timing — after-hours and weekend slots are included, not billed
