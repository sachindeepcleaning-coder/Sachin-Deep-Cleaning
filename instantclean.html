Why It Matters

About Instant & Same-Day Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Same-day cleaning exists for the day the schedule cannot wait — a family arriving with guests in the evening, a flat that has to be shown to a tenant at four in the afternoon, a party that ended at two in the morning, or a home that simply has to be presentable by morning. The point is a fixed four-hour window from the moment the job is confirmed, not a vague promise of same-day service. You call before noon and the crew is on its way within the hour.

We map the flat on the call, decide with you what is in scope for the time available, and send a two to three person crew with a vacuum, a multi-surface mop, a microfibre kit and the bathroom and kitchen chemicals. Bathroom fixtures and the kitchen counter are cleaned first because they carry the most visible result, then bedrooms and living areas, then floors and touch points. The team works room by room and sends you a photo of each finished room before they leave.

The price is ₹999 for the same-day slot and the crew size is decided by the work, not by the clock. The price moves when the home has not been cleaned in a long time and the floors need scrubbing rather than mopping, when there are multiple bathrooms, or when the request is really a deep clean — in which case we quote the deep clean instead and let you choose the date. Nothing is added for the same-day slot itself.

Same-day slots run across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1 and the New Colony apartments, and we cover the office belt in Cyber City and Udyog Vihar, where a client meeting at noon cannot wait. Book by phone early in the morning and the four-hour window holds. If you need more than a surface-level reset, the full home deep clean is the better job.

What is the difference between instant cleaning and a deep clean?

An instant clean is a same-day top-to-bottom reset scoped to a fixed checklist — kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living area, floors and balcony. A deep clean adds machine scrubbing, wardrobe interiors, window tracks and a supervisor walkthrough. The instant package gets you to a clean flat today; the deep clean resets the grout and grime.
