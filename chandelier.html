Why It Matters

About Chandelier & High-Reach Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A chandelier collects dust the way a ceiling fan does, but the dust is on glass, and glass shows every fingerprint of it. Crystal drops dull, brass arms darken, the canopy collects a layer on top, and the bulbs are usually missed for years. The same applies to the high glass and the light fittings in a lobby or a double-height living room. In Gurgaon the dust mixes road particulate with construction fines, so it settles fast.

Every drop is handled by hand. Our cleaners wear cotton gloves and use a dry micro-duster first, so grit is lifted off the crystal instead of dragged across it. The glass is then wiped with a lightly dampened microfibre, using distilled water where available so it dries without spotting, and the brass arms with a matched product. Bulb holders and the canopy are detailed separately, and the fitting is reassembled before it is switched on. Over a staircase or a high ceiling, a boom lift or a pole set-up from the floor is used.

Chandelier and high-reach cleaning is ₹1,499 per unit for a crystal or glass fixture, with high-reach access included. The rate is per fixture, not per bulb, so a heavy multi-arm chandelier in a lobby and a small pendant in a flat are charged the same way; what changes it is the access method and the height, since a 12th-floor balcony fixture needs a rope-access team quoted separately. High glass around a fixture is quoted per pane.

We clean chandeliers and high-reach fittings across the high-rise flats of DLF Phase 5, Nirvana Country, Ardee City and Vatika City, and in the lobbies of offices and hotels on Golf Course Road, Sohna Road and Cyber City. Most clients book the fixtures twice a year, before the festival season and after the monsoon. In a flat with a multi-arm fixture over the dining table, it pairs well with a full home deep clean so the fittings and the rest of the home are done in one visit.

Do you dismantle a chandelier to clean it in Gurgaon?

Only where the fixture can be safely reached and the pieces are individually handled. In most Gurgaon homes the unit is cleaned in place, piece by piece, dried and re-hung. Dismantling is quoted separately because it needs a rigging plan and a longer access window.

How do you reach a chandelier in a double-height living room?

For a double-height lobby we work from the floor with extension poles up to a height, and above that we arrange a lift or rope-access as a separately quoted line. The access quote comes with the clean quote, so you see the full figure before a crew is booked.

Can you clean lobby feature lights in Gurgaon offices?

Yes. Lobby feature and statement lights are survey-quoted because each fixture, height and access path is different, and the same unit in a residential lobby is never priced like a corporate one. GST invoices and after-hours slots are standard for office work.

Is chandelier cleaning worth it before Diwali?

Yes. Festival lighting concentrates on the fixture a guest sees first, and dust on crystal scatters light instead of throwing it. Gurgaon festive slots fill two weeks early, so for a pre-Diwali chandelier clean message by mid-October.

Do you clean chandeliers in societies and villas in Gurgaon?

Yes, in both. A villa or builder-floor unit is ₹1,499 and a society common lobby feature is survey-quoted. Society work needs an RWA window, a GST invoice and a per-fixture tick sheet, which are all included at no extra cost.
