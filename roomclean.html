Why It Matters

About Single Room Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Most homes do not need a full deep clean every month. What they usually need is one room that has got away from them — the bedroom where the clothes pile up, the kitchenette a tenant uses, the bathroom with hard-water marks. Booking a full-home deep clean for that is overkill, and skipping it altogether lets the room set the tone of the whole flat. Single room cleaning is the small, precise version of the same work.

The cleaner starts with the room and a checklist written for that specific room type: vacuum the floor, the bed base or cot, under the furniture, the curtains and the cushions, then dust the walls, the window frames, the ledges and the electronics, then wet-wipe every surface that should not be sprayed. A bathroom room also gets the taps descaled, the mirror and glass wiped, the floor scrubbed and the fittings sanitised. A kitchenette room gets the counter degreased, the sink scrubbed and the bins emptied.

The price is ₹299 per room and the visit takes 60–90 minutes. The price does not change with the room type, but the time does, because a bathroom or a kitchenette is slower than a bedroom. The price changes if the room has not been touched in a long time, if there are stains that need extra dwell, or if you add a mattress shampoo, a window glass clean, a curtain clean or a wall spot clean. Two or more rooms booked together are cleaned in one visit.

Single room bookings run across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1 and the New Colony flats, and we do hostel and PG rooms in these areas on a monthly plan. If the room needs more than a reset, we will tell you on the call and quote a deep clean instead, so you are not paying twice for the same job.

How much does single room cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Single room cleaning in Gurgaon is ₹299 for a bedroom, living room or study, ₹399 for a kitchenette and ₹399 for a bathroom. Two rooms are ₹499. The price is fixed for the room type and confirmed on the call.

How long does one room take to clean in Gurgaon?

A bedroom, living room or study takes 60 to 90 minutes with one cleaner, including dusting, vacuuming, mopping, skirting and the inside of the window glass. A bathroom takes 45 to 60 minutes because the descaling dwell time cannot be rushed.

Is a single room clean worth it, or should I book a deep clean?

For one neglected room, yes — a ₹299 room clean is the cheapest way to fix one bad space. But if several rooms, the kitchen and the bathrooms all need it, the ₹2,500 1 BHK deep clean is better value and covers the whole flat with a supervisor walkthrough.

Do you clean the inside of the windows in a room clean?

Yes, the glass and the tracks are included, because a wiped frame with a dusty track is the most common giveaway of a rushed clean. We vacuum the channel out and wipe the sill and glass as part of the room scope.

Can I book two rooms on the same visit?

Yes, two rooms are ₹499, so a bedroom and a study on the same visit is cheaper than booking them separately. Beyond two rooms, the full-home deep clean usually works out better, and we will say so rather than selling you rooms one at a time.

Do you move furniture to clean a single room in Gurgaon?

We move light furniture — chairs, side tables, a movable stool — to clean under and behind it, and we put it back. We do not move heavy or wall-mounted items, and anything that needs lifting is logged on the tick sheet instead.

Gurgaon me single room cleaning ka price kya hai?

Ek bedroom, living room ya study ki safai ₹299 hoti hai, kitchenette ₹399 aur bathroom ₹399. Do room ek visit me ₹499 lagte hain. Poore ghar ka deep clean ₹2,500 se shuru hota hai.

What changes the single room cleaning price

  • Room type — a kitchenette and a bathroom cost more than a bedroom
  • Room count — the second room is ₹200, beyond two the deep clean is cheaper
  • Condition — a long-unused room needs more hours and a stronger product
  • Windows — inside glass and track cleaning are included in the room scope
  • Furniture — light items are moved and reset, heavy items are not
  • Add-ons — a stain, an odour or a stain-specific treatment is quoted separately
