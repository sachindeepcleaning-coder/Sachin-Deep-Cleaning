Why It Matters

About Sweeper & Mop Cleaning Services in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Sweeping and mopping is the daily hygiene layer of a home or an office, and it goes wrong in predictable ways. A dry broom pushes fine Gurgaon dust into corners and under furniture instead of lifting it, and a mop run with detergent leaves a film that turns grey and sticky within days. Dust that is not captured keeps circulating through the air and settles back on the beds and the cupboards. Done properly, one daily slot keeps a home ahead of the build-up instead of resetting it every month.

A sweeper-mop visit is a fixed sequence. Dry sweeping with a dust mop and a microfibre pad collects the loose grit from the floor, skirting, ledge and the corners under the beds, and a HEPA vacuum follows on carpets, curtains, sofa and mattress edges. Wet mopping comes next with a flat mop and a neutral, pH-balanced solution that leaves no film behind. Entrance mats are machine cleaned, kitchen and washroom surfaces are wiped and disinfected, and the waste is segregated, bagged and taken out.

A sweeper and mop visit is ₹499. Daily and weekly plans are quoted by the area and the number of shift hours rather than by one flat rate, because a 2 BHK needing two hours a day is a different commitment from a studio needing one. That ₹499 covers the crew, the equipment and the products, with no extra charge for our machine. Extra tasks such as window cleaning, fridge interiors or sofa shampooing are booked as add-ons on the same visit.

We run sweeper-mop visits for homes in DLF Phase 1–5, Sushant Lok, South City, Palam Vihar, Sector 45–57, Golf Course Road and Sohna Road, and for offices, clinics, salons and shops in Cyber City, Udyog Vihar and MG Road. The same team returns on each visit, so the routine gets faster and the checklist does not change. After the monsoon, most clients add one deep clean to reset the layers that daily visits cannot remove.

How much does sweep and mop cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Sweeping and mopping in Gurgaon starts at ₹499 per visit for a studio and reaches ₹1,699 for a 3 BHK. Weekly plans work out cheaper per visit than one-off bookings. The price is fixed for your home size, not for how long the visit happens to take.

Is a weekly sweep and mop cheaper than a monthly deep clean?

Yes, per visit — but they do different jobs. A ₹1,199 weekly 2 BHK visit keeps the home at the same standard between visits, so the ₹4,500 deep clean is postponed by months. The plan is maintenance; the deep clean is a reset of the grout, grime and cooked grease a sweep never touches.

Do you bring your own broom and mop in Gurgaon?

We bring a multi-surface mop, a HEPA vacuum, microfiber cloths and eco-friendly sprays. You provide water and power. The bag is the same every visit, which is why the checklist and the quality are identical whichever cleaner is dispatched.

Can I get the same cleaner every week?

Yes. Weekly and bi-weekly plans get the same trusted team each visit, so they learn your home, your floor type and your preferences — and they clean faster over time, which is one reason the per-visit rate drops on a plan.

What is the difference between sweep and mop and house cleaning?

They are the same thing: full dusting, HEPA vacuuming, mopping and floor care, kitchen and bathroom surface upkeep, bin emptying, high-touch sanitization. A sweep-and-mop visit is house cleaning by area — a studio is ₹499, a 3 BHK is ₹1,699, and the visit takes 60 minutes to 3 hours accordingly.

Do you sweep and mop commercial floors in Gurgaon?

Yes — commercial floors are survey-quoted by area and frequency, and scheduled after hours so no workday is lost. Societies and offices on a recurring plan get the same team each visit and a photo trail on request.
