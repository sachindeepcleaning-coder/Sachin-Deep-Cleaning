Why It Matters

About Carpet Shampoo Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Carpets are dust magnets — especially in Gurgaon, where fine particulate and construction dust settle straight into the pile. Everyday vacuuming only lifts the surface; the deep dirt, pet hair, dust mites and spilled stains stay trapped below, dulling colours and triggering allergies. A carpet that looks “okay” at a glance can hold years of embedded soil that only hot-water extraction removes — our vacuum test on first visits across DLF, Sohna Road and Golf Course Road homes routinely surprises owners.

Our carpet shampoo cleaning uses hot-water extraction to flush out deep-seated dirt and allergens, pre-treats stubborn stains and neutralizes odours, leaving your carpet visibly brighter and fresher. We clean wall-to-wall carpets, area rugs and runners, and assess the fibre — wool, nylon, polyester — before choosing the right treatment. Pricing is by area: from ₹15 per square foot for dry cleaning and ₹18 per square foot for shampoo extraction. A large living-room carpet typically dries within 4–6 hours.

Vacuum cleaning services between professional visits keep the surface honest: twice-weekly slow passes (not quick glides) halve the soil load our extraction must lift. Homes with pets or young children should vacuum high-traffic lanes every other day and book extraction every 4–6 months; regularly vacuumed adult-only homes can stretch to 12 months. After renovation dust, flooding or a major spill, book immediately — settled cement and moisture bond fast in Gurgaon’s climate.

Carpet cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹18 per square foot for shampoo extraction and ₹15 per square foot for dry cleaning. A 100 sq ft carpet therefore comes to ₹1,800 for shampoo or ₹1,500 for dry cleaning. Stain treatment and allergen removal are included in both rates, and area rugs start at ₹600 per piece.

How long does a carpet take to dry after shampoo cleaning?

Six to eight hours for a shampooed carpet, and two to four hours for a dry-cleaned one. We run air movers across the carpet while we work and leave them running afterwards, which pulls moisture out of the pile. A room with good ventilation and fans dries faster, and heavy furniture should stay off the carpet until it is dry.

Is carpet shampoo cleaning worth the price compared with vacuuming?

Vacuuming lifts surface dust; it does not remove the soil, hair and allergens embedded in the pile. A shampoo extraction flushes that dirt out with hot water and detergent, which is why carpets visibly brighten and allergies ease afterwards. Vacuum twice a week between services and the extraction interval can stretch to nine or twelve months.

What is included in a carpet shampoo cleaning service?

A carpet shampoo clean includes a dry pass to lift loose soil, pre-treatment of every stain and spill, hot-water extraction of the whole area, a rinse pass, and a sanitising treatment against allergens and dust mites. Furniture is shifted and replaced afterwards, and edge and corner lines receive an extra pass by hand.

Can you remove stains and pet hair from a carpet?

Yes. Fresh spills, oil, ink and pet urine are pre-treated before extraction, and pet hair is lifted with a high-suction pass and rubber brush. Urine odour is neutralised at the padding layer, not just the surface. A stain that has bonded to the backing over a long period may not lift fully, and we say so before starting.

Do you move furniture for wall-to-wall carpet cleaning?

Light furniture — coffee tables, chairs, floor lamps and planters — is shifted and put back as part of the job. Heavy items like beds, wardrobes and dining units are cleaned around unless you arrange for them to be moved. Tell us on the booking call and we plan the crew size and machine path around them.
