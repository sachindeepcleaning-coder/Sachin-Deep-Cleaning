Why It Matters

About Post-Construction Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Construction dust is not ordinary dust. The fine cement particles produced by cutting, drilling and grinding get into grout lines, socket cavities, false ceiling voids, AC grills and every unfinished edge, and they settle again every time the wind moves through a half-open window. A normal mop walks the dust into a film and spreads it. Paint drips on tiles, silicone smears on glass and adhesive residue on the floor need solvents and hand work, so the job has to be done in the right order or the dust simply returns.

We work in three phases. Phase one is the rough stage: rubble, packaging, plaster chips and excess material cleared and removed from the site. Phase two is the high stage, where false ceilings, AC vents, fan blades, ledges, window frames, wall tops and the tops of doors are cleaned with a vacuum and a long-reach tool. Phase three is the final stage: a HEPA fine dust pass over every surface, a scrub of the tiles and grout, glass and frame polish, and the removal of paint marks and adhesive with the right solvent for each material.

The price is ₹2,499 for a 1 BHK up to 600 sq ft, and the work takes 1–2 days depending on the site. The price moves with the number of floors and whether there is working lift access, with paint drips and adhesive that need solvents rather than wiping, with the number of fixtures and whether the kitchen and bathrooms were fitted, and with debris volume in phase one. Debris removal beyond the loose waste is charged separately.

Post-construction cleaning runs in the new societies along Sohna Road, Sector 43, 45, 48, 51 and 57, the DLF Phase 5 and DLF City Phase 4 handover blocks, Golf Course Extension, New Gurgaon and the residential projects in Manesar. We also handle renovation work inside an occupied flat, where the same three phases are applied room by room so the household can stay in the home. Book it before the furniture arrives.

How much does post-construction cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Post-construction cleaning in Gurgaon starts at ₹2,499 for a 1 BHK up to 600 sq ft, ₹3,499 for a 2 BHK and ₹4,499 for a 3 BHK. It is a three-phase job — rough debris, then high surfaces, then a final HEPA pass — and the price covers all three phases on every size.

How long does post-construction cleaning take in Gurgaon?

A 1 BHK takes one day, a 2 BHK one to two days and a 3 BHK two days. The job runs in three phases because construction dust re-settles: clear the rough debris and plaster first, then the high surfaces, then the final HEPA pass so you are not left with a film on the day of possession.

What is the difference between post-construction cleaning and a normal deep clean?

A normal deep clean works on settled household grime. Post-construction cleaning handles construction debris, cement dust, plaster, paint spots and silicone residue, and it needs the phase order so the final HEPA pass happens last. A deep clean on a dusty flat would just move the dust around.

Do you remove construction debris in Gurgaon?

Yes, in phase one — plaster, brick, chippings and the general debris haul, with the disposal arranged through a licensed route. Larger volumes are quoted separately after a survey, because the cost depends on the weight and the material.

Do you remove paint and cement marks from tiles in Gurgaon?

Yes, in the final phase, using a product matched to the surface so glass, vitrified and metal are not scratched. Marks that have etched into the tile itself lighten rather than vanish, and we say so on the walkthrough before you pay.

When should I book post-construction cleaning before possession in Gurgaon?

Book it two or three days before your possession date, once the builder has finished the last cut and the dust has settled. If the builder is still working, we will clean, and the next day the dust returns — so the timing matters more than the booking.
