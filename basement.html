Why It Matters

About Unfinished Basement & Godown Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

An unfinished basement or godown in Gurgaon is raw concrete — no flooring, no paint, no finish — so every speck of dust and every drip of oil goes straight into the slab. After a construction project or a few years of storage, the floor is covered with cement dust and tyre marks, the columns carry paint splashes, and the corners hold bird debris, cobwebs and rodent droppings. Space like this cannot be shown to a tenant or used for storage until the slab and the drains are clean.

We work in three stages: dry sweeping and debris collection first, then a HEPA vacuum pass over the floor, walls and column surfaces, then a machine scrub with a degreaser for the oil and tyre marks that will not lift by vacuuming. Drain lines are cleared and flushed, droppings and organic debris are treated with a sanitising solution, and cobwebs in the ceiling corners come down last. Rubbish and construction debris are bagged and taken off the site.

The rate is ₹12 per sq ft for an unfinished basement or godown, and a 1,000 sq ft space takes 5–6 hours. The price moves with the debris volume we have to remove, the height of the columns and the number of bays, whether oil stains have soaked into the slab and need chemical treatment, and whether access is through a narrow staircase where the equipment has to be carried in by hand rather than wheeled.

We take basement and godown jobs in the industrial belt of IMT Manesar, Udyog Vihar Phase 1 to 5, Sohna Road, Sector 43, 45, 48 and 51, and in the residential basements of DLF Phase 3 and Palam Vihar where society owners want the storage and parking space usable again. The same crew and equipment handle both, so the price does not change with the type of space.

How much does basement cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Unfinished basement and godown cleaning in Gurgaon starts at ₹12 per sq ft, rising to ₹14 for a space with stored material and ₹18 for heavy debris and cobwebs. A 1,000 sq ft godown takes 5 to 6 hours with a two-person crew.

How long does it take to clean a 1,000 sq ft basement?

A 1,000 sq ft unfinished basement takes five to six hours with a two-person crew — sweeping, HEPA vacuuming, corner and cobweb work, then mopping the floor. Heavily stored or debris-heavy spaces take longer, and the time is in the quote rather than an estimate on the day.

Will you move stored goods to clean underneath?

Yes, we move what we can lift and clean underneath, then put it back in the same place. Anything heavy, fragile or stacked higher than the crew can safely handle is left in place and the area around it is cleaned and logged on the tick sheet.

Do you clean basements with damp or mould in Gurgaon?

We clean the surface, but a damp or mould-affected area is survey-quoted separately because it needs drying and remediation before a normal clean will hold. We tell you on the walkthrough what we can treat and what needs a different trade, rather than quoting a clean that will not stick.

Can you clean a commercial godown in Gurugram?

Yes. Godown and basement cleaning is survey-quoted for commercial spaces, and we issue a GST invoice with a photo trail, which is what most facility teams need for their records. Night and weekend scheduling is available so no working day is lost.

Is debris removal included in the basement cleaning price?

No — disposal is quoted separately after a survey, because the cost depends on the volume and the material. We use a licensed disposal route only, and we never dump in a drain, a nullah or a vacant plot. The clean and the disposal are always two clear lines.
