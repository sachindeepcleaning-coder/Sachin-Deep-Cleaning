Why It Matters

About Kitchen Platform Deep Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

In an Indian kitchen the grease does not stay on the counter, it moves. Oil vapour from the stove settles on the underside of the wall cabinets and the back of the platform, then runs down the joint where the counter meets the tile and sets there as a hard black layer. That layer harbours cockroach eggs and smells even when the counter looks wiped. Nobody cleans under the platform edge, which is why the kitchen smells the same after every quick wipe.

We clear the platform, bag the loose items and cover the gas and electricals first. An alkaline degreaser is applied and left to dwell, then the platform top, the edge profile and the joint behind it are scrubbed with a slim brush. The cabinet doors and drawer fronts come off for cleaning, the sink base is pulled out so the underside, pipe joints and wall behind it can be degreased, and the backsplash tiles are scrubbed. Handles and touch points are sanitised at the end.

The rate is ₹999 and the job takes 2–3 hours, covering the platform, the cabinets, the backsplash and the sink base. The price moves with the platform material, since granite and quartz need a different cream and a lighter scrub than a tiled or mosaic platform, with heavy carbon build-up that needs a second dwell, and with a plumbed sink base, which takes longer to pull out and reseal than a modular one.

Kitchen platform deep cleaning runs across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1, the New Colony flats and the society clubhouses. Modular kitchens in DLF Phase 4 and 5 high-rises take longer because the panels are veneer and the edges are sealed. Ask for it together with our kitchen deep cleaning and the hob, chimney and interiors are done in the same visit.

How much does kitchen platform deep cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Kitchen platform deep cleaning in Gurgaon is ₹999 for the platform, edges, backsplash and sink base, ₹1,399 with the cabinet interiors, and ₹1,599 with the chimney filter dismantled and degreased. A full kitchen deep clean is ₹1,500 for the complete scope.

How long does a kitchen platform clean take in Gurgaon?

A platform-only deep clean takes 2 to 3 hours with a two-person crew, because the platform, the edges and the backsplash joint all need separate product and dwell time. Adding the cabinet interiors or a chimney filter takes it to 3 to 4 hours.

Do you clean inside the kitchen cabinets?

Yes, at ₹1,399 including the platform. Cabinets are emptied, the inner shelves and doors are wiped, and the sticky grease film on the vertical panels is removed — the part that is invisible until you open the door and a drop lands on the floor.

Do you dismantle the chimney filter for a platform clean?

Only in the ₹1,599 package, where the filter comes off, soaks in a food-safe degreaser and is degreased before it goes back, with a suction test at the end. A platform clean on its own does not touch the chimney, because a filter job needs its own soak time.

Is a platform clean enough before Diwali in Gurgaon?

For most homes, yes. The platform, edges and backsplash are what you see and what a cook touches daily, and ₹999 handles that. If the chimney has not been cleaned in a year, add the ₹600 filter job — a greasy filter is what pushes the smell back into the kitchen after the platform is spotless.

Do you clean the hob and exhaust as part of a platform clean?

Not in the platform-only package. The hob, grates and exhaust body are ₹1,299 with the platform, or ₹1,500 inside the full kitchen deep clean. They are separated because a burnt burner needs a different product and dwell time from a greasy platform.
