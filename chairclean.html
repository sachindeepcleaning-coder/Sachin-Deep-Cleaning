Why It Matters

About Chair & Recliner Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

An office chair is the least-maintained surface in a Gurgaon home or office: it is sat on, never washed, and it drinks sweat, dust and food from every break. Foam holds that load inside the cushion, where a wipe never reaches, so the seat smells, the backrest darkens and the mesh turns brittle. Extraction resets the seat instead of hiding the problem under a fabric spray.

We vacuum the chair with a crevice and upholstery tool to pull dry dust out of the seams and underside, then pre-treat each stain with a fabric-appropriate solution and let it sit. Hot-water low-moisture extraction flushes the foam, and the dirty water is sucked out rather than left to soak in. Armrests, the under-seat pan, the mechanism housing and the wheels are wiped separately, and directed airflow dries the seat for use the same evening.

The rate is ₹199 per chair and each one takes 45–60 minutes, so a set of four office chairs is a half-day job. The price moves when the fabric is leather or perforated mesh and needs a different treatment, when a spill has dyed the seat and needs extra dwell, when it is a recliner with a powered footrest we check before lifting the back, and when the chair is dragged out from under a desk. A chair and a recliner cleaned on the same visit are charged per seat, with no visit fee.

We clean chairs across DLF Phase 2 and 3 homes, Sushant Lok 1 and Palam Vihar apartments, and the office floors in Cyber City, Udyog Vihar and Golf Course Road, where a shared desk chair is cleaned with the workstation. For societies and clinics we do a batch of seating on one visit so the cost per chair comes down. If the same seats need attention on a schedule, pair this with an office deep clean and the extraction kit comes once.

How much does chair cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Chair cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹199 for an office or gaming chair, ₹249 for a recliner, and ₹349 for an executive leather chair. A stack of four office chairs is ₹599. The price is per chair, confirmed on the call with the chair count.

How long does it take to clean one chair in Gurgaon?

One office chair takes 45 to 60 minutes, including stain pre-treatment, shampoo extraction and drying. A recliner takes about an hour because the foam holds more water and needs a longer extraction. A stack of four office chairs takes an hour for the whole stack.

Do you clean mesh and leather office chairs?

Yes. Mesh is vacuumed and shampooed with a low-moisture method so the mesh does not stretch or sag, and leather gets a leather-safe clean with conditioning — never a fabric detergent, which strips and cracks it over time.

Can you clean chairs in our Gurgaon office in one go?

Yes. Office and cafeteria stacks are priced at ₹599 for every four chairs, and we schedule the work after hours or on a weekend so no desk is unusable. For a whole floor, the count and the slot are confirmed on the call and the price is fixed before we start.

Will my chair be dry enough to use straight after cleaning?

It is usable within a few hours, though fully dry within 8 to 12 hours depending on the foam. We air-move the chair where we can and schedule the work so you are not sitting on a wet chair the next morning. Do not cover it while damp.

Is it worth cleaning office chairs or replacing them?

Worth cleaning when the foam is intact and the problem is dirt, odour or a stain — a shampooed chair looks materially better for a fraction of a replacement. It is not worth cleaning when the foam has collapsed or the fabric has torn, and we will say so on the walkthrough rather than take the money.
