Why It Matters

About Water Tank Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

The tank above your kitchen or below your parking is the least cleaned surface in any Gurgaon home. An overhead tank on the terrace collects monsoon dust, dried leaves, bird droppings and mosquito eggs; an underground pit collects silt, iron sediment and the residue of years of hard water. Both keep feeding the taps, and the first glass out of the tap carries the worst of it. It is also the only fix for weak top-floor pressure, where scale has narrowed the outlet.

Our tank cleaners close the inlet, pump the water down to the sludge line and lift the debris out with a silt net. The walls and base get an alkaline tank cleaner that breaks down algae and greasy film, followed by a descaler that dissolves the hard-water crust — a concrete pit needs a stronger treatment than a plastic tank, so the product is matched to the material. The tank is then rinsed from the top down, disinfected with a food-safe solution, refilled and left to aerate. Motor servicing and float replacement are not part of the clean; we report them and quote separately.

An overhead tank cleaning is ₹399 and an underground tank is ₹999, fixed before the crew arrives. What moves the number is size and depth: a 1,000-litre tank is quoted above it, and a pit deeper than 1.5 metres needs a longer pump-out. Access matters too: a terrace with a clear opening is quicker than a tank behind a lift machine room.

We run tank cleaning across DLF Phase 1–5, Sohna Road, Golf Course Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok, Sector 45–57 and the newer sectors off Dwarka Expressway, and societies usually book it as a cluster day for a whole tower. Monsoon and the weeks before Diwali are the busiest windows, so book early. If a new flat is being handed over, pair the tank service with a full deep clean so the pipes and the storage are reset together.

How much does water tank cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Water tank cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹399 for an overhead tank up to 1,000 litres, ₹599 for 1,000–2,500 litres, and ₹999 for an underground tank. A combined overhead-plus-underground visit is ₹1,299. Prices are fixed on the call before the visit, with nothing added on site.

How often should a water tank be cleaned in Gurgaon?

Every six months for an overhead tank and every twelve months for an underground tank. Gurgaon runs on borewell and municipal mixes, so the TDS loads tanks faster than in softer-water cities. Societies on a single borewell should book a tower-level clean twice a year.

Is overhead water tank cleaning worth it if the water looks clear?

Yes. Biofilm and scale build on the tank walls long before the water looks dusty, and that film is what carries the smell and the bacteria. A clean tank removes the biofilm layer, not just the visible silt — which is why a ₹399 clean every six months beats a repeat of the ₹999 deep clean.

Can you clean an underground water tank in Gurgaon?

Yes. Underground tank cleaning in Gurgaon is ₹999 for a 2–4 hour visit and needs a two-person crew with entry kit. We scrub the walls and floor, wash out the debris, disinfect with a safe contact-time dose, then hand over with a visible water-level check on the tick sheet.

Do you clean society water tanks for RWAs in Gurgaon?

Yes — society tanks are cleaned at tower level, from ₹1,499 per society for an overhead-plus-underground pair. We book within a scheduled RWA window, carry our own entry kit, issue a GST invoice, and hand over a per-tank tick sheet the RWA can file.

What is the difference between a ₹399 tank clean and a ₹999 one?

The difference is the tank and the entry. A ₹399 clean is an overhead tank the crew reaches from a ladder with a brush and a hose. The ₹999 visit is an underground tank that needs entry kit, debris removal, wall scrubbing at floor level, and a longer disinfectant contact time before the rinse.

What changes the water tank cleaning price

  • Tank size — capacity in litres changes crew time and chemical dose
  • Tank type — overhead tanks are reachable, underground tanks need entry kit
  • Scale and biofilm load — a tank that has not been cleaned in two years takes longer
  • Access — terrace height, hatch size and whether a second crew member is needed
  • Society booking — a tower-level clean is priced per society, not per flat
  • Add-ons — insulation jackets and pipe fittings are billed only when requested
