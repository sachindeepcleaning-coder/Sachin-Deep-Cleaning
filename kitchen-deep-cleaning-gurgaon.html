Why It Matters

About Kitchen Deep Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A kitchen is the hardest-working room in any Gurgaon home — and the most neglected during routine cleaning. Daily cooking leaves a stubborn layer of grease on chimney filters, exhaust fans, stove burners, cabinet doors and the tiles behind the hob. That sticky film collects dust and can turn into tough, baked-on grime over just a few months.

Our kitchen deep cleaning service dismantles and degreases the chimney and exhaust fan, scrubs every cabinet inside and out, descales the sink and taps, cleans the stove, hob and microwave, wipes splashback tiles and grout, and finishes with the kitchen floor. We use food-safe, eco-friendly degreasers so your cooking surfaces stay hygienic for your family. A standard kitchen takes 3–4 hours with a 1–2 cleaner team.

How much does kitchen deep cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Kitchen deep cleaning in Gurgaon starts at ₹1,500 for a standard modular kitchen and runs to ₹3,500 for a large or island kitchen. The chimney filter dismantle, filter soak, hob degrease, exhaust cleaning, cabinet interiors, splashback and grout scrub are all inside that figure. An oven interior deep clean is ₹400 extra.

What exactly is included in a kitchen deep cleaning service?

The chimney filter is removed and soaked to release baked-on grease, the hob and exhaust fan are degreased, cabinet interiors are wiped out including the corners where oil mist collects, and the splashback, tiles and grout joints are scrubbed. We finish by wiping the countertop edges, cleaning the sink and sanitising the handles and switches.

How long does kitchen deep cleaning take?

Three to four hours for a standard modular kitchen, and four to five hours for a large one with an island and a built-in oven. Most of the time goes into the filter soak, which cannot be hurried. If the kitchen has not been cleaned in over a year, expect the upper end of that range rather than the lower.

Is the chimney filter cleaning included in the price?

Yes. Dismantling the chimney filter, soaking it in degreaser, scrubbing it and refitting it is part of every standard kitchen deep clean from ₹1,500. The mesh, the grease tray and the duct cover are all handled on site. If the filter needs chemical replacement or the motor is faulty, that is quoted separately.

Do you clean the inside of kitchen cabinets and the fridge?

Cabinet interiors are always cleaned — shelves, corners and the underside of the chimney, because that is where most of the oil sits. The fridge interior is a separate job at ₹500, since it needs the shelves pulled out, washed and dried before the fridge is plugged back in. We empty it only if you have already done so.

How often should a kitchen be deep cleaned in Gurgaon?

Every six to nine months for a working kitchen, and every four months if you fry daily or have an open kitchen facing the road. Between deep cleans, wiping the hob and hob ledge after use keeps the grease from hardening on glass. Gurgaon kitchens collect cooking mist and road dust faster than most, so the range stays short.

How much extra does a kitchen with heavy baked-on grease cost?

A heavily greased kitchen usually runs ₹2,500–₹3,500 instead of the standard ₹1,500, because built-on carbon needs a longer soak and a second degreaser pass. The price is quoted after we see the hob, hood and chimney filter on a call or a photo. We never bill by the hour on kitchen jobs, so the figure holds once quoted.

What changes the final price

  • Kitchen size — standard, large or island layout
  • How long the kitchen has gone without a deep clean
  • Number of cabinets and the amount of internal storage
  • Condition of the built-in oven, microwave and chimney filter
  • Grease and burnt residue on hob, tiles and grout joints
  • Add-ons such as fridge interiors or exhaust duct cleaning
