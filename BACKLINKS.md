## Rules (violating these burns the domain)

1. **Never buy links** (Fiverr/Telegram “DA50 backlinks”) — link schemes draw manual penalties.
2. **Never automate submissions** (bots, spun bios) — inconsistent NAP + spam patterns hurt more than zero links.
3. **One NAP everywhere** — resolve the Sector-43 vs Block-C conflict first (see top).
4. **Anchor variety** — brand (“Sachin Deep Cleaning”) 50%, URL 25%, topical (“deep cleaning Gurgaon”) 25%. Never exact-match 100%.
5. **301 the old domain first** — every new link should point at `.shop`; links to `.online` leak equity until the redirect exists.
