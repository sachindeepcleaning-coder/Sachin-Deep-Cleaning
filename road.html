Why It Matters

About Road & Street Sweeping in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Gurgaon builds fast, and the dust shows up on the road first. Silt washed off open plots and construction sites during the monsoon settles into the kerbs and low spots, tyres grind it into the surface, and by winter the internal roads of a society look grey even after a sweep. There is the everyday layer too — leaves, paper, plastic and the grit cars carry from the main road. Sweeping pushes that grit to the edges rather than removing it, so it returns with the next vehicle.

The work depends on the surface and the area. A wide internal road is swept with a tractor-mounted or truck-mounted suction sweeper that lifts dust into a hopper, while kerb lines, corners and the spaces under parked cars are cleared by hand with a road brush. Where water is available, the surface is dampened first so the dust binds instead of floating. The collected material is loaded, taken out of the site and disposed of properly — not tipped at the corner of the road, where wind would carry it back.

Road and street sweeping is ₹6 per square foot, covering the sweeping, the dust removal and disposal. The cost is driven by the area and the equipment needed to cover it, not by the number of workers. A narrow lane needs a smaller machine and takes longer per square foot than a wide road. Monthly and weekly schedules use the same rate with a fixed service window, and one-off pre-festival or post-monsoon sweeps are booked the same.

We sweep internal roads and driveway aprons in the societies of DLF Phase 1–5, Nirvana Country, Ardee City, Vatika City and the Sector 50–57 sectors, and in the commercial complexes along Sohna Road, Golf Course Road and Cyber City. Longer stretches through an industrial or market lane are quoted after a site visit. Most clients run a monthly sweep, add a pre-festival visit and pair it with a deep cleaning visit so lobby and road reset together.

How much does street sweeping cost in Gurgaon?

Street sweeping in Gurgaon starts at ₹6 per sq ft for a residential stretch and ₹7 per sq ft for a commercial lane, with a minimum booking per visit. A society perimeter and gate road is from ₹4,999 for a half-day stretch, including disposal.

Do you dispose of the swept debris in Gurgaon?

Disposal is arranged only through a licensed route and is quoted separately on the survey, because the cost depends on volume and type. We never dump in a drain, a nullah or a vacant plot — that is the single biggest compliance line on any road-sweeping booking.

Do you sweep service roads and construction lanes in Gurgaon?

Yes. Construction lanes run at ₹8 per sq ft because cement dust and silting need mechanical sweeping followed by water, not a dry brush that just moves the dust into the drain. We schedule after the crew peak so the lane is passable the same day.

Can a society book a road clean for a cluster day in Gurgaon?

Yes, and societies book a perimeter-and-gate clean as a single stretch from ₹4,999 for a half day. We confirm the stretch on WhatsApp, share crew names for the RWA gate pass, and hand over a photo trail the committee can file.

How often should a society road be swept in Gurgaon?

Weekly for a gate road on an active construction corridor and monthly for an internal lane. Gurgaon dust loads the stretch within days, and the drain edges are the first place silt collects — we clean them every visit and flag blockages on the tick sheet.

Is street sweeping cheaper on a monthly contract?

Yes — a monthly stretch contract is priced per visit at a lower rate than a one-off, and the crew learns the stretch, the drains and the access windows. The rate is fixed per visit so the RWA budget is predictable.

What changes the road and street sweeping price

  • Stretch area — the per-sq-ft rate scales with the length and width
  • Surface type — kachcha, concrete and tarmac each need a different pass
  • Dust type — construction lanes need water and mechanical, residential do not
  • Disposal — volume and material decide the licensed-disposal cost
  • Access window — commercial lanes need night or early-morning slots
  • Frequency — a monthly stretch contract is cheaper per visit than one-offs
