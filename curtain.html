Why It Matters

About Curtain Vacuum & Shampoo Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Curtains are the largest soft surface in most Gurgaon homes and almost never get cleaned. Dust from open windows, road grit from Sohna Road and Golf Course traffic, cooking smoke from the kitchen and pollen all settle into the pleats, where they stay until the fabric darkens, the colour fades unevenly and the room starts smelling of old cloth. A dusty curtain also holds the heat in summer and lets the dirt back through the moment the window is opened.

We take the panels down, vacuum both faces and the top pleat with an upholstery tool, then pre-treat the marks along the header and hem. Each panel is shampooed with a low-moisture extraction and hung in a steamed state so it dries stretched rather than creased. We rehang everything on your existing rods and tracks, adjust the drop, and check the hooks and gliders while we are up there. Rings and rods are wiped down at the same time.

The rate is ₹499 for a set of up to four panels, and a full home with three or four sets takes 3–4 hours depending on the window size and how high the rods are. The price moves with the fabric, since sheers and lightweight voile need a gentler treatment than blackout or velvet panels, with floor-length windows that need a ladder, and with the number of windows in the home, which is charged per set. Combining curtain cleaning with carpet shampoo on the same visit is the cheapest way to do both.

We take curtain cleaning bookings across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1 and the New Colony flats where high ceilings make the reach the deciding factor. Homes in the taller societies along Sohna Road usually book it before Diwali, when the dust of the last few months comes out of storage.

How much does curtain cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Curtain cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹499 per set of up to four light panels and ₹699 when a wet shampoo is needed. Heavy or blackout fabric is ₹899 per set. A curtain-plus-sofa set is ₹999 for one visit.

Are curtains cleaned on site or taken away in Gurgaon?

On site. We vacuum and treat the curtains where they hang, then leave them to dry in place, so there is no waiting period and no risk of lost or damaged panels. Heavy blackout fabric takes longest to dry and is quoted accordingly.

How long do curtains take to dry after cleaning?

Light fabric dries in four to six hours, and heavy blackout fabric takes eight to twelve. We schedule so the windows are usable and you can close them for the night. We never rehang a curtain that is still damp.

Does curtain cleaning remove the smell of damp or smoke in Gurgaon?

Yes, usually. The smell sits in the fabric, the dust that has worked into the pleats and the accumulated body oil along the top edge, and a deodorising pass with a proper extraction removes it. Where the odour is in the fabric itself and cannot be lifted, we say so before you pay.

How often should curtains be cleaned in Gurgaon?

Every six to nine months for normal use, and every four to six for a home near a construction site or a main road, where the dust load is much higher. It also depends on whether you run the AC on a window seat, which pulls more dust into the pleats.

Do you clean the curtain rod and track as well?

Yes, at no extra charge as part of the set clean. The rod and track collect more dust than the fabric and are the first thing you see when the curtain is closed, so we wipe them down as part of the job rather than charging a separate line.

What changes the curtain cleaning price

  • Fabric weight — blackout and heavy material needs a slower, wetter process
  • Cleaning method — a dry deodorise is cheaper than a wet shampoo
  • Number of panels — priced per set of up to four
  • Drying time — heavy fabric is quoted at more time because it dries slowly
  • Access — high or awkward windows change the time on site
  • Rod and track — cleaned as part of the set, never billed separately
