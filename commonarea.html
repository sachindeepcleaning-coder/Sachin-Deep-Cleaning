Why It Matters

About Common Area Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A society’s common areas are used by everyone and owned by no one. Dust settles in the lift lobby, leaves pile up in the corners of the stairs, cobwebs appear above the false ceiling in the lobby, and the parking apron collects oil marks and tyre dust. Residents notice it first when guests come over, and it drags the look of the whole tower down. That is why the best-kept homes in Gurgaon often sit in buildings where common areas are maintained on a schedule instead of fixed after a complaint.

A common area visit runs with industrial equipment, not a household kit. The crew starts at the top floor and works down, so dust falls once. Lobbies and corridors are dry swept with an industrial broom and vacuumed, cobwebs are cut down with extension lances, and the lift lobby and entrance mats are machine cleaned. Staircases get a solution-feed scrub on the treads and a separate mop for the landings. The parking apron is pressure washed where the surface allows it, leaves and paper are gathered, and bins are washed and sanitised with fresh liners.

Common area cleaning is ₹499 per visit, covering the lobby, the stairs, the lift lobby, the parking apron and the clubhouse. Daily and weekly schedules are quoted by area and shift hours rather than a flat per-visit number, because a 4 BHK tower and a 32-floor tower are not the same job. A periodic deep scrub — lifting ingrained marks out of a tiled lobby or the black build-up on stair nosings — is quoted separately.

We run common area contracts across DLF Phase 2–5, Sushant Lok 1–3, Nirvana Country, Ardee City, Vatika City, South City and the Sector 50–57 societies, and the same crew returns on the same day each week so residents learn who to reach. Cluster days work well for towers, where several blocks are cleaned in sequence. If the flats behind the doors also need work, our deep cleaning services cover them the same week.

How much does common area cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Common area cleaning in Gurgaon is ₹499 for a lobby and lift lobby visit, ₹299 per floor for a staircase and corridor, ₹799 for a parking apron, and ₹2,499 for a full tower visit. RWA bookings are invoiced with GST and a per-zone tick sheet on completion.

Can an RWA book a single tower clean, or do they need the whole society?

A single tower is fine and is often the better start. One tower common-area clean is ₹2,499 for a half-day visit covering lobby, stairs, lift lobby, apron and clubhouse. We pre-share crew names for the gate pass, work inside the RWA window, and hand over a tick sheet you can file.

Do you clean society parking aprons and podiums in Gurgaon?

Yes — aprons and podium edges are pressure-washed at ₹799 for a two-hour visit. Drain edges and glass balustrades are included, and we flag blocked drains on the tick sheet rather than clearing them, which is a separate trade.

How do you schedule around a society that is always occupied?

We book the RWA window you set, split the work zone by zone, and carry our own entry kit and IDs. Lobby work happens before the morning peak and apron work after the evening one, so residents are never blocked. The same ₹499 lobby rate applies either way.

What is included in a tower common-area clean?

A half-day tower visit covers the lobby and reception, every lift lobby, staircases and corridors, the parking apron edge and the clubhouse common block. Glass, floors, railing, high-touch points and cobwebs are in scope. Repairs, plumbing and drain clearing are not, and are listed as exceptions.

Do you issue a GST invoice for RWA bookings in Gurgaon?

Yes, on completion, with a per-zone tick sheet and the crew names that worked each zone. RWA committees use it for the maintenance trail, and it is included at no extra cost — the price on the call is the price on the invoice.

Gurgaon me society common area cleaning ka rate kya hai?

Lobby aur lift lobby ki safai ₹499 per visit hoti hai, ek floor ka staircase ₹299, aur parking apron ₹799. Ek poore tower ka common-area clean ₹2,499 se shuru hota hai, jismein GST invoice aur tick sheet milta hai.

What changes the common area cleaning price

  • Zone count — lobby, stairs, lift lobby, apron and clubhouse are separate lines
  • Floors in scope — staircase and corridor work is priced per floor
  • Surface type — glass balustrades and vitrified floors need a different chemistry
  • Society access — gate passes, lift slots and the RWA window are confirmed on the call
  • Frequency — a weekly common-area visit is quoted per visit, cheaper than one-offs
  • Extras — gate-pass handling and the tick sheet are included, not billed
