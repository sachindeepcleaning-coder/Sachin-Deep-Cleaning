About Deep Cleaning Rate Per Square Foot in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

Most cleaning quotes in Gurgaon are priced per BHK, which is convenient until two flats with the same label turn out to be very different sizes. A "2 BHK" can be 600 sq ft or 1,000 sq ft, and the work is not the same in either. The per-square-foot rate removes that ambiguity: the price is the measured area multiplied by one rate, which does not move with your floor plan.

We measure on the walkthrough, room by room, and write the number on the quote before anything starts. Carpeted and tiled areas that we clean are counted, including under a fitted wardrobe and the utility floor if it is in scope, and the kitchen and bathroom tiles are included because they are the heaviest part of the job. Both are on the confirmation call, so the invoice matches what you agreed to.

At ₹3 per sq ft, a 1 BHK of about 450 sq ft comes to ₹1,350, a 2 BHK of about 850 sq ft to ₹2,550 and a 3 BHK of about 1,250 sq ft to ₹3,750. The fixed BHK card is ₹2,500 for a 1 BHK, ₹4,500 for a 2 BHK and ₹5,500 for a 3 BHK, and it bundles in work the sq-ft rate does not price — wardrobe interiors, the balcony, window tracks, ceiling fans and appliance interiors. The sq-ft rate is therefore cheaper on a compact home or a partial scope, while the card is worth it for a full reset in one line.

Both prices work the same way across DLF Phase 2 and 3, Sector 43, 45 and 57, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, Palam Vihar, Sushant Lok 1 and the New Colony blocks — we carry the measuring tape, not a label. Send us the carpet area from your brochure and we will tell you which price applies to your flat, and you can compare it with our full home deep cleaning scope before you decide.

What is the deep cleaning rate per square foot in Gurgaon?

Deep cleaning in Gurgaon works out to about ₹3 per square foot, pro-rata from a fixed BHK card: 1 BHK ₹2,500, 2 BHK ₹4,500, 3 BHK ₹5,500, 4 BHK ₹6,500 and 5 BHK or villa from ₹9,000. The BHK figure is what we quote, because a per-sq-ft rate alone hides what is included.

Is the per-square-foot rate cheaper than the BHK card?

On a 1,300 sq ft flat the BHK card usually costs less than the same scope priced by area alone, because a pure area-based rate does not price wardrobe interiors, balcony drains, window tracks, fan and AC vent detailing or appliance interiors. The saving is real, but it is a scope difference, not a discount on the same work.

How do you calculate the price for my home in Gurgaon?

We count built-up area from your floor plan or the photos you send, then map it to the BHK band. A 1 BHK at 400 to 600 sq ft is ₹2,500, a 2 BHK at 601 to 1,000 sq ft is ₹4,500, and a 3 BHK at 1,001 to 1,500 sq ft is ₹5,500. The figure is locked on the call before the crew is booked.

Why do you quote by BHK instead of by square foot?

Because area does not predict the work. Two 900 sq ft homes can differ completely: one has three bathrooms and an empty kitchen, the other has one bathroom, a furnished bedroom and a heavy kitchen. The BHK card plus the condition of the home is what predicts the hours, so that is what we price on.

What changes the price if my flat is furnished?

A furnished home adds ₹500 to ₹1,500 and one to two hours, because wardrobe interiors, sofa detailing, under-bed and behind-furniture work take real time. We confirm it from the photos before quoting, so the figure you get is the figure you pay on the day.

Does the per-square-foot rate include the same checklist?

Yes, when you book the BHK card — the scope is identical, only the way it is expressed differs. If a competitor quotes you a lower per-sq-ft number, ask what is in it: wardrobe interiors, balcony drains, window tracks, fan and vent detailing, or appliance interiors are where the real differences sit.

What changes the deep cleaning price per square foot

  • Built-up area — the rate is pro-rata from the BHK band
  • Number of bathrooms — a third bathroom is a real descaling cycle
  • Furnishings — wardrobe interiors and under-furniture work add ₹500–₹1,500
  • Condition — a long gap since the last clean needs more hours
  • Surfaces — marble and Kota need pH-matched chemistry, vitrified does not
  • Access — floor, lift and parking conditions affect the crew-hours
