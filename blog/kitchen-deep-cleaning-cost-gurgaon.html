Why Gurgaon Kitchens Need It More Often

  • Daily cooking with mustard oil/ghee → sticky grease on chimney, tiles and cabinet tops

DIY vs Professional — When You Need Experts

Monthly mesh rinse (hot water + baking soda) is good DIY maintenance. Professional degreasing is needed for: baked-on grease, baffle/cassette filters, hob under-body, cabinet exteriors and tile grout. Our food-safe commercial degreasers dissolve layers that home products cannot.
