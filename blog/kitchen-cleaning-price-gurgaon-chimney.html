Why Chimney Degreasing Changes the Whole Quote

  • Baffle filters — degreased in a chemical bath on site; 20–30 minutes of soaking beats an hour of scrubbing.
  • Filterless models — the oil cup and turbine housing are the real work; both are in the quote.
  • Ducted vs ductless — ducted hoods get the duct opening wiped; ductless units get a charcoal-filter assessment (replacement is extra).
  • Neglected chimneys — a year of grease needs two bath cycles; the ₹200–₹300 surcharge is quoted before work starts, never after.

A properly degreased chimney makes the whole kitchen easier to keep clean — less grease re-settles on cabinets and tiles each week. That is why the chimney is done first, while its chemical bath does the waiting, and why the quote lists it separately: it is the item most crews quietly skip.
