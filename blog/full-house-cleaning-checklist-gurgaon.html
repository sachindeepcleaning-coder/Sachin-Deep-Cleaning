Gurgaon homeowners searching full house clean, full house clean checklist or full house deep cleaning checklist want a printable, landlord-proof list — not a sales pitch. Here is the actual checklist our team uses for 1–5 BHK homes across DLF, Sohna Road and Golf Course Road.

Full House Cleaning Checklist — Room by Room (Printable)

Bedrooms & Living Areas (All)

  • Floors — scrub, polish, corners, under-bed
  • Walls & ceiling — dust, cobwebs, light fixtures, fan blades
  • Furniture — wardrobe exteriors & shelves, cupboard detailing
  • Windows & sills — glass inside, tracks vacuumed, sills wiped
  • Switchboards & door handles — disinfected

Kitchen (The Critical Room)

  • Chimney — filter dismantled, degreased, suction tested
  • Hob & burners — dismantled, burnt-oil scrubbed, traps cleared
  • Cabinets & drawers — interiors & exteriors degreased
  • Countertops & tiles — splashback grout scrubbed
  • Sink & taps — descaled, exhaust fan dismantled & cleaned
  • Floor — degreasing scrub

Bathrooms (Each)

  • Tiles & grout — descaled, scrubbed with stiff brush
  • Faucets & shower head — limescale dissolved, wrapped cloth treatment
  • Commode — bowl, rim, seat & base sanitized
  • Basin & mirror — polish & drain clean
  • Shower glass — hard-water film removed
  • Exhaust fan — grill & blades
  • Floor — scrub & disinfection

Balcony, Terrace, Hallways & Windows

  • Floor — scrub, drain de-clog
  • Railings & parapet — pigeon-dropping disinfectant
  • Glass & sliding tracks — vacuum then wipe
  • Hallways & passages — wall spot-clean
  • Main door & handles — disinfected
