Why It Matters

About Kitchen Utensils Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

The inside of a kitchen is where cooking oil collects and polymerises. A kadhai used for frying every evening develops a black carbon layer that flakes into the food, a tawa holds burnt residue around the base, and a pressure cooker collects scale around the gasket and vent. Stainless steel picks up water spots and heat tint that dull the whole shelf. These are not problems you can wipe away — the carbon has to be broken down chemically and then physically, a different job from wiping a counter.

We sort the vessels first: stainless steel, aluminium, non-stick, brass and glass each need their own treatment. The job starts with dry scraping to lift off loose burnt flakes, then the utensils go into a hot alkaline degreaser bath that loosens the polymerised oil. Carbon is worked off with a stainless scrubber on hard surfaces, while non-stick pans get a non-abrasive method that will not scratch the coating. Brass and copper get a gentler product, gaskets and vents are cleaned with a small brush, and everything gets a hot-water rinse and air dry.

Kitchen utensils cleaning starts at ₹409. The number depends on the load: a typical set of around 20 pieces — a kadhai, a tawa, two pots, a pressure cooker, plates and cutlery — sits at the starting rate, while a kitchen with 40 or more vessels is quoted on count and carbon. What moves the price fastest is the carbon: a vessel never cleaned needs the bath time and scrubbing it cannot get in a sink.

We clean utensils for homes across DLF Phase 1–5, Sohna Road, Golf Course Road, Sushant Lok and the Sector 45–57 societies, and for PG hostels and small commercial kitchens in MG Road and Cyber City. Most people book it with a kitchen deep clean so the chimney, cabinets and vessels are done in one visit. Before Diwali, when the full kitchen set comes out of the cupboards, this is the season’s most requested job.

How much does kitchen utensil cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Kitchen utensil cleaning in Gurgaon starts at ₹299 for a cutlery and serving-set wash, ₹409 for a daily lot of up to 20 pieces and ₹699 for a standard kitchen lot of 20–50 pieces. A heavy lot of 50 or more vessels is ₹999. The figure is confirmed on the call after the item list, never revised on site.

Do you clean brass, copper and silver vessels in Gurgaon?

Yes, with metal-safe products rather than a generic detergent — brass and copper are treated for tarnish and depth, silver with a safe polish that does not strip it. If a piece is lacquered or needs a specialist polish, we tell you on the walkthrough before proceeding.

Do you clean inside pressure cooker lids and gaskets?

Yes. The lid, the gasket, the vent pipe and the regulator come apart where they safely can, so the starch film and the oil film under the gasket are removed. That part is the one a hand wash never reaches, and it is what carries the stale smell.

Can I book a utensil clean on its own, or is it part of a kitchen clean?

You can book it on its own. A standalone daily-lot utensil clean is ₹409 (₹299 for a cutlery and serving-set wash) and takes 60–90 minutes — useful before Diwali or after a large function when the kitchen is stacked. If the cabinets, chimney and splashback also need a reset, the ₹1,500 kitchen deep clean is better value.

Do you dry and pack the utensils after cleaning in Gurgaon?

Yes — drying, stacking, and packing back into the storage is included at no extra charge. We dry every piece on a rack, pack them into the same cupboard section, and hand over with a count so nothing is short and nothing is wet where staff would walk.

How long does a utensil clean take for a family kitchen?

A typical family lot of 20–50 pieces takes 90 minutes to two hours with a two-person crew. A heavy lot with large storage containers and brass vessels takes two to three hours. The timing is part of the quote, not an estimate on the day.
