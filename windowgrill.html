Why It Matters

About Window Grill Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

The window grill is the most neglected surface in a Gurgaon flat. It sits outside, so it collects road dust, pollen, dead insects and bird droppings, and the mesh behind it becomes a filter that blocks the airflow. Dust also works into the joints and sliding channels, where it grinds the frame every time the window moves. On floors facing Golf Course Road or the open plots around Sohna Road, the deposit is heavy enough that the grill reads grey, not black.

Grill cleaning needs more than a wet cloth, so we bring a mesh brush and a wet-vacuum setup that pulls dust out of the grid instead of pushing it around. The grill is dry brushed first, then the mesh is washed with detergent while the vacuum holds the dirty water at the frame bottom, and the frame and channels wiped. Droppings are treated one by one with a spotting solution so they do not etch the powder coating, and light rust on the joints is treated too. The sill is cleaned and dried before the window closes.

Window grill cleaning is ₹399 per window, covering the grill, the mesh and the sill. The rate is per window, not per square foot, because a wide bedroom grill and a narrow kitchen window take the same time. What changes it is access: a window reachable from inside the room is quicker than one needing a balcony ladder or service balcony, and a grill on the fourth floor or above with no service balcony is quoted after a site visit.

We clean window grills across DLF Phase 1–5, Sushant Lok, Palam Vihar, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road and the Sector 45–57 societies, and in offices along Cyber City and Udyog Vihar where frontage grills take traffic dust. Most people add it to a quarterly deep clean so grills, windows and balconies are done together. Grills in high-rise flats are best cleaned before the monsoon, when the mesh is dry and the dust has not turned to grit.

How much does window grill cleaning cost in Gurgaon?

Window grill cleaning in Gurgaon costs ₹399 per window for the grill bars and corners, ₹499 with the mesh screen washed, and ₹549 including the sill and outer track. A full window clean combining grill, glass and tracks starts at ₹799.

Do you dismantle window grills to clean them properly in Gurgaon?

Where the grill can be safely disassembled, yes. The bars are scrubbed on both faces and the recessed corners that never get a cloth through are reached with a brush. Grills that are bolted into the frame are cleaned in place and logged on the tick sheet so you know the difference.

Why do window grills get dirty so fast in Gurgaon?

Because construction dust from the corridors films the bars within weeks and the horizontal ledges hold it. The hard-water mist from balcony drying deposits a mineral film on top. Both are physical deposits that a dry dust just moves around, which is why a wet scrub works and a wipe does not.

Can you treat a window grill that has started rusting?

Yes, at ₹149 per grill. We scrub the corrosion, apply a rust-treatment product and then a protective finish where the surface allows it. Grills that are beyond treatment are flagged honestly on the walkthrough rather than repainted to hide it.

Do you clean window grills before Diwali?

Yes, and it is one of the highest-value pre-Diwali items because the grill frames the window. Book it before the full-home clean so the final walk-through shows clean bars. Gurgaon festive slots fill two weeks early, so message by mid-October.

Is it cheaper to book a full-home clean than a grill-only clean?

It can be. A grill-only clean is ₹399 per window, so a three-window flat bills ₹1,197. If the glass, tracks, kitchen and bathrooms also need a reset, the ₹2,500 1 BHK deep clean covers the grills and everything else, which works out cheaper overall.
